RedMetal Player Ultimate Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026

RedMetal Player Ultimate is a desktop video player for Windows. This privacy policy explains what data the app handles and how it is used.

Personal Data Collection

RedMetal Player Ultimate does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal information for advertising or profiling.

Local App Data

The app may store settings and local usage data on your device, such as theme preferences, playlists, playback progress, subtitles, viewing statistics, and achievement progress. This information is used only to provide app features and remains on your device unless you choose to share files or data yourself.

Media Files

Video and subtitle files selected in the app are opened locally on your device. The app does not upload your local media library to the developer.

Watch Party and Network Features

If you use watch party, local streaming, or tunnel features, the app may create a temporary local or internet-accessible connection so other participants can connect to a session. These features are optional and may use third-party network infrastructure, such as Cloudflare tunnel services, to route a temporary connection. Participants who receive a session link may access the shared stream while the session is active.

Third-Party Services

Optional tunnel features may rely on Cloudflare services. Your use of those services may be subject to Cloudflare's own privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

RedMetal Player Ultimate is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app plays media selected by the user and does not provide curated content.

Data Security

The app is designed to keep settings and playback data locally on your device. When using sharing or tunnel features, only share links with people you trust.
